Operations & Physics Reference

A reference card for the mathematical modeling and equations that power GhostTrack's solver and propagator.

Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN)

Traditional AI needs thousands of labeled images. GhostTrack relies on a PINN that directly embeds momentum-transfer dynamics into the loss function.

  • Pre-impact momentum: We track the satellite's nominal TLE orbital path before the collision event.
  • Conservation of momentum: mdeb · vdeb + Msat · vsat,0 = mdeb · v'deb + Msat · v'sat
  • Sensor telemetry: Attitude changes (Δωx,y,z) allow us to resolve the debris' offset impact leverage arms.

Trajectory Propagation Math

Once debris velocity is estimated, we propagate its orbit using Runge-Kutta 4th Order (RK4) integration, factoring in gravitational and atmospheric perturbations.

  • J2 Oblate Earth Gravity: Account for Earth not being a perfect sphere, causing orbital precession.
  • Atmospheric Drag: adrag = -0.5 · Cd · (A/m) · ρ · v · v
  • Uncertainty Cloud: Monte Carlo perturbation of initial conditions maps 3D danger margins for 30 days.
